//Without collapse, only the outer loop is parallelised, giving 5 units of work. 
With 10 threads available, 5 threads receive one row each and the remaining 5 are idle; each working thread executes its inner k loop serially.

//With collapse(2), the perfectly nested loops are merged into a single iteration space of 5 × 10 = 50, which is then distributed across the team. 
All 10 threads receive 5 iterations each, and a single value of r may be handled by more than one thread. 
This increases parallelism and improves load balance where the outer loop count is smaller than the thread count.
